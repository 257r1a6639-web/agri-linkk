Our Services

AGRI LINKK brings agricultural participants together through digital services designed to simplify farm information, product discovery and connections between farmers and businesses.

What We Provide

A connected platform designed for farmers, businesses, logistics providers and communities.

Farmer Registration

Farmers can create accounts and maintain their basic profile and agricultural information.

Farm Management

Keep digital farm records with details such as land area, location, crop name and farming method.

Product Listing

Farmers can list agricultural products with quantity, description, grade and price.

Digital Marketplace

Eligible users can explore agricultural products listed on the platform and discover available offerings.

Grade-Based Pricing

Organize product listings by quality categories such as Grade A, Grade B and Grade C, with corresponding prices where applicable.

Logistics Discovery

Logistics participants can explore agricultural listings and relevant farmer information to identify potential connections.

How Our Services Work

Four simple steps to get started with AGRI LINKK.

1

Create an Account

Register with your basic information and select the appropriate account role.

2

Add Information

Provide relevant farm, product or organization information.

3

Explore Products

Browse agricultural product listings and available platform information.

4

Build Connections

Connect with relevant participants and explore opportunities to collaborate.
