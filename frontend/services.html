Our Services

AGRI LINKK provides digital services that make it easier to discover agricultural products, connect participants and manage farm information.

What We Provide

A connected platform designed for farmers, businesses, logistics providers and communities.

Farmer Registration

Farmers can create accounts and maintain their basic profile and agricultural information.

Farm Management

Store land area, location, crop name and farming method in a digital farm record.

Product Listing

Farmers can list products with quantity, description, grade and price.

Digital Marketplace

Eligible users can explore agricultural products available on the platform.

Grade-Based Pricing

Products can be categorized into Grade A, Grade B and Grade C with their respective prices.

Logistics Discovery

Logistics participants can view available agricultural products and farmer details.

How Our Services Work

A simple four-step digital process.

1
Create Account

Register using your basic information and role.

2
Add Information

Enter farm or organization details.

3
Explore

Browse products and available agricultural information.

4
Connect

Contact relevant participants and build connections.
