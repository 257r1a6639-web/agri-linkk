Who We Are

We aim to make agricultural connections simpler, more accessible and more organized through technology.

Who We Are

AGRI LINKK is a digital agricultural platform designed to simplify connections between farmers and organizations looking for agricultural products.

Farmers can maintain their farm information and list products with details such as crop type, farming method, quantity, grade and price.

Businesses, logistics providers, communities and apartments can use the platform to discover available agricultural products.

One platform for agricultural connections

What We Do

We provide a common digital space for participants across the agricultural supply ecosystem to connect and discover opportunities.

The platform is designed to support product listings, grade-based classification, role-based dashboards and user authentication.

Our goal is to make agricultural product information easier to discover and help participants find relevant connections.
