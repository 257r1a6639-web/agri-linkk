About AGRI LINKK

Building a connected agricultural ecosystem where farmers, businesses, logistics providers and communities can discover and connect with each other.

Who We Are

AGRI LINKK is a digital agricultural platform designed to simplify connections between farmers and organizations that require agricultural products.

Farmers can maintain their farm information, list products and provide details such as crop type, farming method, quantity and product grade.

MSMEs, logistics providers, communities and apartments can discover available products through the marketplace.

What We Do

Our platform provides a common digital space for participants in the agricultural supply ecosystem.

AGRI LINKK supports product listing, grade-based classification, role-based dashboards and secure user authentication.

The goal is to make agricultural information easier to discover and create direct connections between participants.

Our Vision & Mission

Creating a more connected and digitally accessible agricultural ecosystem.

Our Vision

To build a connected agricultural ecosystem where farmers and buyers can discover opportunities through simple and accessible digital technology.

Our Mission

To provide a platform that helps farmers showcase their products while enabling businesses and communities to discover agricultural products efficiently.
