Powerful Features for Smart Agriculture

AGRI LINKK provides digital tools that connect farmers, businesses, logistics providers and communities through one simple agricultural platform.

Key Features

Everything needed to make agricultural product discovery and connections simpler.

Direct Connections

Farmers can showcase their products and connect directly with MSMEs, logistics providers and communities.

Grade-Based Pricing

Agricultural products can be classified as Grade A, Grade B or Grade C with a corresponding price and quantity.

Secure Authentication

Login and role-based access help users reach the dashboard and services relevant to them.

Responsive Design

The platform is designed using Bootstrap so pages can adapt to laptops, tablets and smartphones.

Digital Farm Records

Farmers can maintain information about land, crop, location and farming method digitally.

Product Marketplace

Eligible users can view available agricultural products with quantity, grade, price and farmer information.

Logistics Connection

Logistics users can discover available products and identify farmers who may require transportation support.

Farming Method Details

Product information can include whether farming is identified as Organic or Pesticide-based.

Admin Management

Administrators can monitor registered users and farmer products through a dedicated dashboard.

How AGRI LINKK Works

A simple digital workflow from registration to agricultural product discovery.

1
Register

Create an account and select the appropriate user role.

2

4

Security & Access Control

AGRI LINKK uses authentication and role-based access to separate the functionality available to different participants.

JWT Authentication
Secure token-based access for authenticated users.
Password Protection
Passwords are protected using secure hashing.
Role-Based Dashboards
Users are directed to dashboards based on their role.
Protected APIs
Backend routes verify authentication before providing protected data.
